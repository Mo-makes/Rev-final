Livery design competition

Design a look for Carrera.

REV is planning a livery design competition for its November general body meeting. Use the Livery Lab to try ideas on the 3D model, then save an image or copy a link to share what you made.

Designs made here are concepts. They are not Carrera’s final livery.

For competition details, email ricerev@gmail.com or follow @rice.rev on Instagram.

How to design

  1. Pick a pattern. Each one paints the body sides, nose, tail, pods and canopy fairing. The solar array on top always stays as cells.
  2. Choose a base and an accent colour. Use the swatches, or open the colour picker for any colour.
  3. Add a race number. Up to three digits, shown in a roundel on both sides. Turn the REV wordmark on or off.
  4. Add your own artwork if you like. Size and place an image on the sides. It never leaves your device.
  5. Check every angle. Use the view buttons to look at both sides, the nose, the tail and the top.
  6. Share it. Save an image, or copy a link that opens the Livery Lab with your design.
