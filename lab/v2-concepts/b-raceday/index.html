Formula Sun Grand Prix 2027

Rice's only student-built wheel-to-wheel racing team

Rice Electric Vehicle (REV) is designing and building Carrera, a solar race car, to take it racing at Formula Sun Grand Prix in July 2027.

Scroll to go green

Sector 2

From sunlight to 75+ mph

A 1000 W solar array feeds the battery, and an inverter drives a hub motor in a rear wheel. Aero simulations put Carrera's top speed at about 70 to 80 mph. In testing, the team hopes to hold more than 35 mph on solar power alone.

Sector 3

A circuit that climbs about 1,200 feet

The FSGP 2027 track has about 1,200 ft of elevation change, closer to the Nürburgring than a flat oval. Climbs drain the battery and descents give some back, so telemetry and models choose an efficient pace for every part of the lap.
